{%- comment -%} Resolves page_title and section for the layouts: the sidebar entry's title when the page is listed in _data/nav.yml, otherwise the page's own title. section is the nav section the page belongs to, used by the top bar and sidebar. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign page_title = page.title | default: site.title -%} {%- assign section = nil -%} {%- for s in site.data.nav -%} {%- for p in s.pages -%} {%- if p.path == page.path -%} {%- assign page_title = p.title -%} {%- assign section = s.section -%} {%- elsif p.match and page.path contains p.match -%} {%- assign section = s.section -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%}
